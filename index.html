Manifesto

Every great interface orbits a system. We built the light around it.

01Design Systems

  1. Pull a curve, get a whole scale. HALO builds 11 perceptual OKLCH steps from one primary. Change the hue and every token linked to it updates.

  2. Alpha ramps tied to your primary, so overlays, hovers and focus rings never drift off-brand. They stay readable on light and dark.

  3. Linear, radial and conic gradients built from your own tokens. Edit a color in the scale and every gradient that uses it updates instantly.

Grid

Picture a vertical beat running through every component.

01Systems · Grid

  1. Set a 4 or 8 pt rhythm once. Type, icons and components snap to the same vertical beat, so every screen keeps its tempo.

  2. Columns aligned to your baseline and subline. No manual math: HALO computes gutters and widths for every breakpoint.

  3. A spacing scale generated from your base unit, named, documented and exported as tokens your developers can use as-is.

A hundred and forty icons. One keyline. Zero compromise.

02Icon Library

  1. Every icon is drawn on a 24 px grid with 2 px of padding and optical keylines: circle, square, portrait and landscape. That's why the set sits evenly in a row.

  2. One slider changes the stroke of the whole library, from hairline to bold. Toggle round or sharp terminals to match your type.

  3. Search by name or meaning. Click any icon to copy clean SVG that inherits currentColor and your stroke token.

Your entire typography universe, built in one place.

03Font Builder

  1. HALO Geometric is a parametric face. Weight, width, slant and terminals are live parameters, not separate files. Shape it, then export.

  2. A math-based type scale centered on your body size. Choose a ratio or a curve; your body text stays the focal point.

  3. Display, heading, body and caption styles are generated and paired, with line-height and tracking tuned for each size.
